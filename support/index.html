Support

Need help with the POD GOD Android app? Email support@podgod.ca.

For a faster answer, include:
  • your phone model and Android version;
  • the POD GOD version shown by Google Play;
  • what you expected and what happened;
  • whether the issue involved Android Auto, Bluetooth, a download, or a particular podcast.

Please do not send passwords, payment information, or private audio.

Privacy requests

You can also use the support address for privacy questions or deletion requests. See the Privacy Policy for details.
