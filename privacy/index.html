POD GOD

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains how POD GOD Inc. ("POD GOD", "we", "us") handles information when you use the POD GOD Android application and related support services.

The short version: your subscriptions, queue, listening history, downloads, settings, and soundbites stay on your device. POD GOD has no user accounts, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. Network requests are made when needed to find, fetch, play, download, or share podcasts and when you choose to request a new Personality.

Information kept on your device

The app stores podcast subscriptions, episode and queue records, playback position, listening history and statistics, download state, preferences, cached artwork and public Personality data, downloaded audio, user-created soundbites, an optional on-device transcription model, and limited redacted error diagnostics. These records are not automatically uploaded to POD GOD.

Android may include eligible app data in a device backup according to your operating-system and Google backup settings. Files you export, save to your media library, or share are handled by the destination you choose.

Network requests needed for podcast features

When you search or listen, the app may connect to Apple’s iTunes Search service, podcast publishers and their hosting or content-delivery providers, and POD GOD’s public data service. Those services receive normal internet request information such as your IP address, user agent, requested URL, and request time. A podcast audio URL can include measurement or advertising redirects controlled by its publisher. Their privacy practices, not this policy, govern their processing.

Personality requests and notifications

If you choose to request a Personality, the app sends POD GOD the requested name, any optional note, a random app-installation identifier, your platform, and whether you asked for a notification. If you request a notification and grant notification permission, the app also sends an Expo push token, which is processed using Expo’s notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android. We use this information only to review the request, show its status, avoid duplicate submissions, and deliver the requested update.

Our service and hosting providers may process IP addresses and ordinary server logs for security and reliable operation. Request records are retained while they are needed to administer the request service, resolve abuse, and meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete a request using the contact information below.

Soundbites, transcription, and sharing

Soundbite editing and transcription run on your device. If you enable captions, the app downloads an open-source speech-recognition model from Hugging Face; your audio is not sent there for transcription. When you choose Save or Share, Android and the app or service you select receive the media and any caption you choose to share.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for podcast and public-data requests, notifications for playback and optional updates, a foreground media-playback service for audio that continues outside the app, wake controls for reliable playback and downloads, and add-only media-library access when you choose to save a soundbite. POD GOD does not request access to your microphone, contacts, precise location, or complete photo, video, or audio libraries.

Sale, advertising, and analytics

We do not sell personal information. The app does not include an advertising SDK or an analytics SDK. Podcast episodes may contain advertising inserted by their publishers, which POD GOD does not select based on app-tracked behaviour.

Retention and deletion

You can remove subscriptions, queue items, downloads, and listening-history entries in the app. You can remove all app-local data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. Shared or exported files must be deleted from their destination separately. To request deletion of a Personality request or related server-side information, contact us and include the requested Personality name and approximate submission date.

Security and international processing

We use encrypted HTTPS connections for POD GOD-operated services and reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information. No system is completely secure. Third-party podcast URLs supplied by publishers may use their own connection and security arrangements. Service providers may process information outside your province or country, where it may be subject to local law.

Children

POD GOD is a general-audience podcast player and is not directed to children. Podcast content comes from independent publishers and may include mature topics.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above.
